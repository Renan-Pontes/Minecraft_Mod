## What it does
- **Encyclopedia**: a section of the B menu's Guia de Viagem, next to the Diário (no new top-level tab). 85 entries in 8 categories, all open except 7 "Verdades Ocultas", each unlocked by completing a fragment collection.
- **Lost Pages**: 37 fragments in 8 collections, read in order within a collection. Pages are injected at load time into every `isekai_mahou:chests/*` table (20%) and 18 vanilla chest tables, and are guaranteed in ruin chests and treasures.
- **Ruins of Velmora**: 6 small variants in stone and sandstone palettes, each with one inscribed stele and a loot container. Reading a stele gives a fragment once per player per stele; player-placed steles are blank, so they cannot be farmed.
- **Reading reveals a place**: the nearest matching structure, boss lair, or a buried treasure. The player gets one chat line with coordinates, a tracker arrow, and the position stored in the Encyclopedia with "Rastrear" and "Procurar de novo".
- **Treasure**: the barrel is only buried when the player arrives, in natural ground, outside any registered place, and never in `home_realm`.
- **Balance**: 40 XP per fragment, 250 per collection, modest loot.

## How to test
Run `/mahou admin login` first if the gate is on.
1. Open B → Guia de Viagem → Enciclopédia.
2. `/mahou lore page`, then use the item: the page screen opens, a chat line gives coordinates, and the tracker arrow appears.
3. `/mahou lore page smuggler_1`, read it, then `/mahou lore goto smuggler_1`: within about 10 blocks a "Cave em X Y Z" message appears. Dig there for the barrel.
4. `/mahou lore ruin` teleports to the nearest ruin (new chunks only). Right-click the glowing stele.
5. `/mahou lore unlock all` unlocks everything, including the Hidden Truths, without locating places; `/mahou lore unlock sages_1` does one with locating.
6. `/mahou lore status` and `/mahou lore reset`.
7. `/loot give @s loot isekai_mahou:chests/mage_tower` a few times to see the injected page.
