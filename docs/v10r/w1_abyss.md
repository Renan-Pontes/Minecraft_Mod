- **Endless Abyss:**
  - Entry and arena: entered from the B menu tab at level 100+. Each run gets its own arena in a void dimension, built once per slot and reused.
  - Floors: waves grow with the floor (+10% health, +4% damage per floor); every 5th floor is a boss.
  - Rewards: a little XP every floor; shards and mana crystals on boss floors; on a first-time 10-floor milestone, an Abyss Core plus a rotating rare item and a new starting checkpoint.
  - Leaving: dying, leaving, logging out or a server restart returns the player to where they entered, at half health or more, with no item loss.
  - Co-op: a party mate (level 100+) who clicks Enter during the pause between floors joins the same run, up to 4.
  - Screen: one boss bar and short action-bar lines only.
- **Shadow Fights:**
  - Where: every 5th floor spawns the Shadow of a boss someone in the run has beaten; the tab also lists defeated bosses with a "Fight" button for a single fight.
  - What a Shadow is: the real boss entity, stronger, renamed "Sombra de X", with a purple outline and a darkened body.
  - Rewards: its own only (fragments, shards, XP, and a Core on the first win per boss); it pays no normal boss loot, boss XP or `boss.*` counter.
  - Excluded bosses: `trial_avatar`, `primordial_dragon`, `depths_worm_king`, `star_devourer`.
- **Balance guard:** common Abyss mobs lose their inflated health at death, so the core's kill XP matches the normal creature.

**How to test in game** (after `/mahou admin login`)
1. `/mahou balance` and `/mahou balance 95 105` — XP table.
2. `/mahou level 100`, open B → Adventure → "Abismo"; the tab should be hidden at 99. Click "Entrar — andar 1".
3. Or bypass the level: `/mahou abyss enter` or `/mahou abyss enter 9`.
4. In a run: `/mahou abyss skip` (clear current wave/boss), `/mahou abyss floor 10` (jump), `/mahou abyss status`.
5. `/mahou abyss shadow ignis` (any boss id, tab-completes) — Shadow Fight without having beaten it.
6. `/mahou abyss setbest 20`, reopen the tab — "Continuar — andar 21" should appear.
7. `/mahou abyss leave` and `/mahou abyss record` work for any player.
8. Die inside (`/kill`), and relog inside — both should return you to the entry point.
